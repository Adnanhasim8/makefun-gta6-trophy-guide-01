      How this guide was built

      Every trophy name, description and grade is the official PlayStation 5 list, as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles — the two agreed on all 55 before this guide was written. The list is 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze.

      Each guide is written for this entry, in our own words, from the official description. Where a guide gives a place or a step, it says so plainly when that step has not yet been verified in the game: the game is days old and no guide here pretends to have played what it has not. A cited guide beats a confident uncited one.

      Using the tracker

      • Checklist: tick trophies as you earn them; progress saves in this browser (localStorage) the moment you tick.
      • Counters: trophies PlayStation tracks by number (250 stunts, 100 vehicles…) have a counter — tap + as you go; reaching the target ticks the trophy.
      • Spoilers: the spoiler setting hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
      • Export / Import: export downloads your progress as a JSON file; import restores it on any device.
      • Offline: this app loads nothing from the network. It works with the network off.
