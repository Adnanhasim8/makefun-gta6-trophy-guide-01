    The road to platinum

    PowerPyx rates the platinum 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours, in a single playthrough — 55 offline trophies, no online ones, no mission replay and no New Game+. The order below follows their roadmap: story first, then the 100% checklist, then the leftovers. Two rules cover the whole run: keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter, and keep cheats off — cheats disable trophies and saving.

    Roadmap facts: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap. Trophy names and descriptions: the official list via Exophase.

      Missables — and the ones that only look missable

      Good news first: according to PowerPyx there is only one potentially missable trophy in the whole list. Everything else is either an automatic story trophy or stays available in free-roam after the story, because there is no mission replay but also no trophy locked behind a one-time mission. The table below is built from the official descriptions plus PowerPyx's guide.

      Potentially missable

        One-shot conditions — retryable, but do them carefully

        These trophies ask for something in a single attempt. Failing the attempt does not lock you out: you can try again. They are listed here so you do not waste a run on a sloppy first try.

          Automatic story trophies — nothing to miss

          Every mission-named trophy pops for finishing its mission. Optional missions with an hourglass icon can expire, and some quest givers go quiet for a while, but PowerPyx confirms all trophy-related characters come back later, so no story trophy can be missed.

Their guides say so plainly and give only what the official description itself states, rather than guessing a location or a trigger.

            How the tracker works

            • Ticking a trophy saves it in your browser (localStorage) — nothing leaves your computer, and the app makes no network request at all.
            • Export progress downloads your ticks as a JSON file; Import progress reads one back.
            • Hide story spoilers replaces the name and description of story trophies you have not earned yet with a placeholder, until you earn them.
            • Filters cover kind (story, side questlines, collectibles, activities, robberies, open-world misc, 100% completion), grade, earned state, and the missable flag. There are no online trophies in this list, so no online filter is needed.
